01 — Context

RedPeak advises consumer brands on positioning and go-to-market. Every engagement starts the same way — market analysis, competitive benchmarking, big-data insight, then reporting and a presentation — and in 2024 that front half was eating most of the calendar.

02 — Role

I joined as a strategy intern and ended up owning two things: a share of the strategy work itself across 14 projects (research, benchmarking, LinkedIn content strategy, bilingual articles and translation), and the internal tooling that made that work faster.

03 — Approach
1
Find the repeated work

Interviews with consultants surfaced the same tasks in every project: summarizing briefs, pulling competitor positioning, drafting first-pass insights.

2
Build a research assistant

A GPT-4 chatbot grounded on project briefs, brand trackers and market reports, so a consultant could ask a question instead of re-reading a folder.

3
Add concept visuals

A Stable Diffusion image-generation platform for mood-boards and concept variants — a draft in minutes rather than a design round in days.

4
Ship it into the workflow

Rolled out across live projects with the team, measuring turnaround before and after.
