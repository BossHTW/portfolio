01 — Context

In September 2026 shoppers started arriving as agents: Meta’s Muse and OpenAI’s Dots browse, compare and check out for a person, and they filter out anything over budget before a human ever sees it. A store with no agent of its own simply never gets a word in.

02 — Role

Solo, in one day at The AI Commerce Gallery: research on agentic commerce and the sponsor platforms, the product and brand, the build, and the pitch deck, one-pager and a sourced investor memo.

03 — Approach
1
One room per negotiation

Each deal opens a shared Deal Room on Band. Every agent has a stable @handle, and work moves by @mention, so the shopper’s agent and the store’s agents meet on neutral ground.

2
A team, not a chatbot

A Concierge speaks for the store, a Scout verifies the shopper’s competitor claim on the live web, Inventory knows stock and how long an item has sat, and a Pricing Critic sets the floor and can veto.

3
A person only when it matters

Anything below the floor pings the owner on WhatsApp with the margin and the reason. One tap approves or declines, and the room carries on.

4
Every move on the record

Every message and tool call is a timestamped event, so the room log is the audit trail a merchant would ask for.
