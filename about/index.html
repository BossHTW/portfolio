About

Homeschooled in Taiwan, studying business and computational sciences in San Francisco, and unable to leave a problem at the whiteboard.

At a glance San Francisco, CA · from TaipeiMinerva University, B.Sc. ’27English & Mandarin, native Résumé →

I’m Boss Huang (黃柏實). I grew up homeschooled in Taiwan, which meant I spent my teens doing the things a timetable would have crowded out: founding a STEAM nonprofit, teaching IoT and CAD to adults at the Ministry of Labor, curating TEDxNTUST, and building a lot of hardware — lightsabers, a mechanical heart, a smart mirror, laser-cut butterflies.

In 2021 I was selected as the youngest Youth Affairs Commissioner in New Taipei and learned what it takes to turn a policy argument into a NT$130M budget line and, eventually, a city bureau. Since then the projects have moved from the workbench to the market: a blockchain sustainability platform, a Japan expansion plan for a restaurant chain, AI tooling for a brand consultancy, a North America campaign for a Korean food company.

The constant is the same in all of it. I like the part where research has to become a decision, and the decision has to become a thing that works. I’m finishing a B.Sc. in Business & Computational Sciences at Minerva University, and I teach coding, robotics and Mandarin to kids in the Bay Area on the side.
