01 — Context

Most hobby lightsabers are either a toy or a very expensive prop. I wanted the third thing: a saber you could actually duel with, built from what was lying around, and controllable from a phone.

02 — Build

Version 1.0 (2018) modeled a film-prop hilt from recycled pipes, air-conditioning foam and 3D-printed components. Version 2.0 (2020) replaced the hand-wired electronics with a custom PCB — smaller, sturdier, and with wireless app control for color, effects and sound.
