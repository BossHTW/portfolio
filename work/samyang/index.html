Samyang Foods — a North America campaign built from 8,500 posts

Sentiment analysis of 8,500+ consumer posts turned into a 2.5M-reach campaign concept, five localized ad concepts, and an influencer plan with 7.8M combined followers.

01 — Context

Samyang’s noodles were already a North American phenomenon; the question was how to grow from a viral product into a brand people had opinions about. The job was to find out what those opinions were, and build a campaign from them.

02 — Role

Marketing intern on the North America team in Seoul: consumer research, concept development, creative collaboration and the influencer plan.

03 — Approach
1
Listen

Social media sentiment analysis of 8,500+ North American consumer posts to identify flavor preferences and brand-perception gaps.

2
Concept

Led concept development for a digital campaign projected to reach 2.5M target consumers across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

3
Localize and test

Worked with the creative team to produce five localized ad concepts for US and Canadian audiences; A/B tests on mock ads projected a 23% CTR improvement.

4
Amplify

Recommended influencer partnerships with a combined 7.8M followers, estimated to lift brand awareness in key markets by 30% within the campaign run.

04 — Outcome
+23%projected click-through improvement from A/B mock-ad testing
2.5Mtarget consumers across three channels
+30%estimated brand-awareness lift from the influencer plan

Campaign creative and internal data are confidential to Samyang Foods.
