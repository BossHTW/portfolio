01 — Context

After years of hosting and organizing beach cleanups, a pattern was hard to ignore: environmental movements weren’t sustainable. Most were one-off, in-person events — the beach was clean for a weekend, and then everyone went home.

02 — Approach

Start where people already are. In 2018 I designed a bilingual Line sticker set — the hermit crab characters lived in the chats people opened every day. In 2020 our team published the picture book the stickers came from, then built a program around it: 3R smart-home IoT workshops, online webinars, a curated online exhibit, and the #SaveTheEarthChallenge social campaign.
