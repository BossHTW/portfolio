01 — Context

Laya Burger is one of Taiwan’s largest breakfast-and-burger chains, with hundreds of franchise locations at home and an executive team weighing a first move into Japan. The open questions were practical: where to land, how to feed the stores, and whether the unit economics survive import costs.

02 — Role

I owned the research and the recommendation end-to-end as a logistics intern: market sizing, competitor benchmarking, supply-chain modeling, consumer research, and the final bilingual deck presented to the executive team.

03 — Approach
1
Map the market

Market research across six major Japanese cities, compiling 120+ competitor pricing data points to locate the price band Laya could own.

2
Model the supply chain

Analyzed five candidate supply routes against a baseline import model; the preferred route reduced estimated logistics cost by 18%.

3
Listen to the customer

350+ online survey respondents and 15 in-person stakeholder interviews, folded into the concept and the site criteria.

4
Make it decidable

A 40-slide bilingual expansion deck, shortlisting three prime locations with projected first-year revenue of about NT$45M.

04 — Outcome
−18%estimated logistics cost vs. baseline import model
3prime locations shortlisted for the executive team
~NT$45Mprojected annual revenue in the first year of launch
